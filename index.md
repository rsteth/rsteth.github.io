## Museum of Other Realities

I started the Museum of Other Realities as a social space for creatives prototyping new forms of cultural production. The museum was the official virtual platform for Cannes XR, Tribeca, NewImages, and VIFF Immersed. MOR was also a finalist for Immersive Reality Technical Achievement at the D.I.C.E. Awards.

https://museumor.com

## Market River

Generative art driven by live stock market data. At the open, midday, and close, market and volatility moods become a river landscape through versioned templates, not ad-hoc prompts. Every image traces back to the market snapshot that made it. One visual style, systematically varied.

[Read more](https://stethem.com/market-river/index.md)

## Great Circle

A cycling loop planner: pick a start point and distance, get a near-circular route, and export it as GPX for your bike computer.

https://stethem.com/great_circle/

## Verbosele

Wordle with levels. Solve words from 5 to 9 letters across five rounds on one shared pool of 18 lives, with optional thesaurus hints. Made for my mom.

https://stethem.com/verbosele/

## Focus Speed Reader

A browser speed reader for EPUBs and plain text. Words flash one at a time at the WPM you choose, and your library stays on your device.

https://stethem.com/focus-speed-reader/
